Te Whare Hukahuka is a Māori governance institute based in New Zealand.

We help Māori organisations govern well – developing leaders, creating methodologies and frameworks, sharing insights and convening collaborations that strengthen governance, strategy, measurement and succession from a Māori perspective.

Our leadership team is currently in the UK and Europe (Aug–Sep 2026) to explore conversations around governance, leadership, organisational development and community impact.

A different way of seeing organisations

The Māori worldview treats these six disciplines as deeply interconnected and essential to enable an organisation to successfully endure over time. Te Whare Hukahuka works at this intersection.

Governance

Holding the whole system with clarity and accountability.

Leadership

Leading from service, values and whakapapa.

Strategy

Navigating towards a shared vision across generations.

Culture

Living our identity, language and values in all that we do.

Stewardship

Caring for people, place and resources for future generations.

Impact

Understanding what truly changes for our people and our world.

Indigenous knowledge in practice

Observation:

Indigenous wisdom often draws on observations from the natural environment and the use of metaphor to develop frameworks that transfer knowledge over generations. This retains context and holistic relationships. They may appear simple, but they hold significant depth and nuance.

Below are four examples of indigenous Māori frameworks which are proven to enable governance leaders to run more effective organisations.

Whare Tupuna Governance Framework

A framework linking eight dimensions of effective Māori governance to the whare tupuna – elevating the role of governance beyond compliance to stewarding kaupapa (intergenerational agendas).

Open full-screen ↗

Strategic Planning Poutama

A pathway to develop robust organisational strategy that sets out the strategic direction and the organisation’s core identity, and then moves through a set of strategic choices to build focus, alignment and a rigorous assessment of what it will take to ensure effective delivery over time.

Seasonal Governance

Why the UK & Europe?

The simple answer is that we are coming to the UK and Europe to build relationships.

We believe that some of the most important ideas actually emerge when different traditions of knowledge come together.

Through our work with hundreds of organisations we've been able to observe patterns. And we’ve been able to garner insights.

Across the UK and Europe we are aware of some extraordinary universities, communities and Indigenous organisations, philanthropic groups, social enterprises, governance practitioners who are all doing really important work and asking equally important questions.

We look forward to sharing in wānanga on our shared interests.
